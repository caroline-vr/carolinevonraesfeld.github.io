welcome to my website!

I'm a PhD candidate at Northwestern University studying galaxies at Cosmic Noon (z~2-3). I'm interested in the stars and gas inside them, their chemical makeups and how that affects the physics going on inside these galaxies. I use emission-line spectroscopy from JWST and Keck/MOSFIRE as a part of the CECILIA survey.
