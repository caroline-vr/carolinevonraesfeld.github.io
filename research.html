research

I'm interested in using the UV to the NIR to understand the stars and interstellar medium in galaxies at Cosmic Noon (z~2-3). I love atomic physics and astronomy, so it's a perfect little niche! I'm a part of the CECILIA survey

  • (ApJ focus issue)
    • , a Cycle 1 JWST program aimed at calculating direct electron temperatures and densities from faint rest-optical auroral lines in a sample of galaxies at Cosmic Noon. The CECILIA galaxies, notably, have subsolar S/O and Ar/O, indicating that Type 1a enrichment has not yet become a major player in chemical enrichment of these galaxies.

    In my work, I investigate the applicability of our current photoionization models to interpret Cosmic Noon galaxy emission. Our galaxies show quite strong emission from low-ionization ionic species of singly-ionzied Sulfur and neutral Oxygen.

    I'm broadly interested in stellar nucelosynthesis and the processes that make elements.
