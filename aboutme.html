about me

My love of astronomy started in Northern California, where I grew up. In high school I volunteered as a docent at the Robert Ferguson Observatory in Sugarloaf State Park. There, I got to run the CCD and take pictures of galaxies lightyears away and share them with the public. The clear night sky on the mountain is still one of my favorite sights.

I went to the University of California, Los Angeles for my undergraduate, where I got a Bachelors of Science in Astrophysics and a Minor in Professional Writing. I have always loved the humanities in addition to physics, and I was able to take courses in narrative writing, public-facing science writing, and legal writing while taking quantum mechanics. I graduated Phi Beta Kappa, Eta chapter of UCLA. There, I was also a part of the Astronomical Society at UCLA, where we hosted star parties on top of the Math-Science building and organized events to get undergraduates interested in research. I was also an editor at the UCLA Undergraduate Science Journal, which kicked off my interest in scientific publishing.

In my career I have maintained my interest in astrophysics and the humanities, and I am now getting a certificate in Media and Science Communication from Northwestern's Medill School of Journalism.

I am very interested in the history and philosophy of science! Why do we study what we study? How has what we studied changed, and how does what we study affect society?
